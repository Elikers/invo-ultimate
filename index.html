Exact experimental rules and validation limitations

ADX/DMI: completed 5M Wilder ADX(14) ≥22, with +DI leading for LONG and −DI leading for SHORT. Adverse sweep: reject a LONG after a recent upward liquidity-rejection wick or a SHORT after a downward rejection wick in the prior four completed 5M candles. Each wick must exceed the preceding twelve 5M bars and close back inside with reversal body. ATR regime: completed 5M ATR(14) divided by the average true range of preceding 30 completed bars must be between 0.65 and 1.70. Combined: require all three gates.

An unavailable indicator rejects an experimental signal rather than silently passing. Baseline outcomes are classified at the original setup time using only completed candles. A filter's kept baseline-trade P&L is not a realizable filtered-strategy P&L; the full replays account for changed eligibility and sizing. Stops and take profits are evaluated using 1M OHLC and favor the stop if both are touched within a minute. This cannot prove real execution or intraminute ordering.

Binance futures tests are cross-venue proxies and do not reconstruct Hyperliquid fills, queue priority, or exchange-specific fees. The cost-stressed scenario doubles estimated fee and slippage. These experiments use previously inspected data and an underpowered chronological split; independent unseen data is required before strategy promotion.

Interpretation safeguards and limitations

Replay rules, caveats and data limitations

Hyperliquid mode retrieves 72 hours of 1M BTC candles, plus 7 days of context. Binance prices, liquidity and funding differ from Hyperliquid; cross-venue findings are NOT INVO execution validation. Requires complete chronological coverage. All indicator states use candles closed at or before the signal time. After an eligible confirmation, the hypothetical fill occurs at the next minute open; exits are assessed against subsequent minute highs/lows. If both SL and TP are touched within one minute, stop is assumed first and that ambiguity is counted. One position at a time.
