# INVO Ultimate v10 — MAHORAGA Adaptive AI Mission

**Status: vision and machine-readable research contract created. No adaptive model is trained or deployed.**

## Founder's aspiration
> Build one of the world's best adaptive trading AI systems, eventually capable of sustained positive net profitability across changing market conditions.

This is an **aspirational long-term goal, not a promise**. Financial markets contain unavoidable uncertainty; no legitimate architecture can guarantee that every trade or every market will be profitable, and “greatest” needs independent, apples-to-apples benchmarks.

## The eighth engine
**MAHORAGA** will be a research-only supervisory AI evaluating seven *separate* expert learners: Fusion, Sweep, Auction, Pullback, Volbreak, Mean and Strict. It must be allowed to **abstain**. It can update research versions when registered forward evidence shows improvement, and roll back when performance deteriorates.

## Success criteria rather than win-at-all-costs
1. *Primary*: independent forward **net** expectancy after verified fees, funding, slippage and spread, with uncertainty bounds and adequate prospective sample size.
2. *Risk*: explicit drawdown, tail-loss, concentration and leverage constraints; never double stakes to recover a loss.
3. *Benchmarks*: compare every individual learner and MAHORAGA against unchanged production engines and simple baselines on identical future timestamps.
4. *Research honesty*: zero historical lookahead, no protected holdout training, count skipped trades and missed polls, correct for correlated markets and repeated candidate selection.
5. *Deployment*: no auto promotion or live orders; the current production seven-engine rules and all risk parameters remain frozen pending separate authorization.

## What a loss should teach
A loss is a single uncertain observation, **not a command to alter thresholds**. With enough recent matched data and appropriate uncertainty checks, models may learn that an engine is less suitable under a predefined regime. An underperforming candidate can be retired, and safe abstention may outperform aggressive action. No model may treat historical re-labeling as new forward success.
