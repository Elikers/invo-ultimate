# MAHORAGA v10.6: no-promotion challenger screen
This implementation is a **candidate evidence gate**, not active self-learning. Baseline v10.3 observations and labels have training_eligible=false; funding, actual fills, and independent attestation are missing. Therefore no candidate train or promotion is allowed. The gate checks minimum distinct runs, days, complete cost profitability, and safety conditions, then only produces a review-required result. It cannot change champion weights, production strategies, or trading parameters. Automated candidate training remains **blocked** until verified eligibility and a separately designed safe training/replay pipeline exist.
